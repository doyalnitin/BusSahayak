The Problem

Millions can't book a bus ticket

India has 12 million+ visually impaired citizens. Existing bus apps like RedBus and AbhiBus have complex visual seat maps, tiny buttons, and zero voice-first UX. Blind users literally cannot book a bus ticket independently.

Our Solution

BusSahayak — बस सहायक

  • Voice-First Navigation — Say "search buses" to start, "book" to reserve, "next" for more options
  • Screen Reader Optimized — Full VoiceOver/TalkBack support with ARIA labels and live announcements
  • Text-First Seat Selection — Seat maps as readable lists, not visual grids
  • Haptic Feedback — Vibration cues for every action, never silent

Accessibility

Built for screen readers first

Always Audio

Never silent. Every action, loading state, and result is read aloud. Silence = crashed for blind users.

48dp Touch Targets

Every button meets WCAG 2.2 minimum touch target size. No accidental taps.

Focus Management

Programmatic focus on screen transitions. Modal focus trapping. Logical reading order.

Step Progress

"Step 2 of 5: Choose Seat" — announced at every transition, not just visual dots.

High Contrast

7:1 contrast ratio. Dark and light themes. Respects system accessibility settings.

Live Announcements

Dynamic content updates announced via accessibilityLiveRegion. Errors linked to fields.
