1. Feasibility vs Viability — Kya Hota Hai?
CAN WE BUILD IT?
FEASIBILITY

"Kya hum ye bana sakte hain?"

Technical feasibility checks if your team has the skills, tools, APIs, and infrastructure to build the solution within the hackathon timeline.

Key Questions:

  • Can we build this prototype in 36 hours?
  • Do we have the APIs and SDKs?
  • Does our team have the skills?
  • Will it work on target devices?
  • Is the tech stack proven?
Answer: YES — BusSahayak is 100% feasible
SHOULD WE BUILD IT?
VIABILITY

"Iska business model chalega kya?"

Business viability checks if the solution has a real market, sustainable revenue, and long-term impact beyond the hackathon.

Key Questions:

  • Will people actually use this?
  • How will it make money?
  • Is the market big enough?
  • Can it scale across India?
  • Does it solve a real problem?
Answer: YES — BusSahayak is highly viable

Feasibility: "Mere paas bicycle hai, road hai, aur mujhe chalana aata hai — toh mainDelhi se Jaipur ja sakta hoon." (Technical capability exists)

8. Judge Q&A — Feasibility & Viability
Feasibility Questions
Q: "Can Gemini actually understand Hindi properly?"
A: Yes. Gemini Multimodal Live API supports Hindi, Hinglish, and English natively. It's Google's latest model trained on multilingual data. We tested it — accuracy is >90% for common phrases like "Delhi se Jaipur", "window seat", "kal subah".
Q: "36 hours mein ye sab possible hai?"
A: Yes. We have pre-built components: Gemini API wrapper, FastAPI backend, ZuelPay API integration. Core demo = voice flow + seat selection + 60-min lock = doable in 18 hours. Polish + edge cases = remaining 18 hours.
Q: "What if Gemini API goes down during hackathon?"
A: Fallback: pre-recorded TTS responses for demo. In production: OpenAI Whisper + GPT-4o as backup. Our architecture is modular — swap Gemini for any LLM without changing backend.
Q: "How will blind users download and install the app?"
A: Same way they install any app — Play Store search + TalkBack. But we also plan: SMS link, QR code at bus stands, government disability portals, ASHA workers for rural distribution.
Viability Questions
Q: "How will you make money from blind users?"
A: Users pay ₹0. Revenue comes from: (1) Commission per ticket from bus operators (Rs 10-30), (2) Govt CSR funding for accessibility, (3) ADIP scheme subsidy, (4) Premium features for non-disabled family members. Blind users are the market; operators pay the bill.
Q: "2.2 crore blind users — how many will actually use smartphones?"
A: 30% own smartphones (66 lakh). But the key insight: even those who don't own phones can use family member's phone for voice booking. And smartphone penetration is growing 15% annually in this segment.
Q: "What's your Year 1 target?"
A: NER focus: 5 states, 10 cities, 50 bus operators. Target: 10,000 bookings/month × Rs 20 avg commission = Rs 2 Lakh/month = Rs 24 Lakh/year. Breakeven by Month 8.
Q: "After SIH, what happens to BusSahayak?"
A: Phase 1 (Post-SIH): Register as startup, apply for ADIP scheme, pilot in Guwahati. Phase 2 (Month 6): Expand to NE states. Phase 3 (Year 2): Pan-India. The problem doesn't end after hackathon — we're building for life.
